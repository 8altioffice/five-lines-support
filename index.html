Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my game progress?

You can clear your game data by uninstalling and reinstalling the application. Note that this will erase your high scores and current progress permanently.

The game is lagging or crashing.

Please try closing other background apps and ensure you have the latest version of Five Lines installed. If the problem persists, restart your device.

How do I turn off the sound/music?

You can toggle sound and music in the Game Settings menu within the app.
